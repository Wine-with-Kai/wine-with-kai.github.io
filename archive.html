Eight bottles, six vintages, one grape. The valley’s icons from the Atlas Peak heights to the Rutherford benches, poured blind and set out by vintage, from a mature 2012 to the ripe 2019s. A single grape read across a decade of Napa.

Bordeaux Right Bank

Pomerol versus Saint-Émilion

Merlot and Cabernet Franc · Blind

Eight bottles, four vintages, the two great Right Bank neighbours set directly against each other in matched pairs and poured blind. Pomerol’s iron-rich clay against Saint-Émilion’s limestone, judged glass by glass across 1986, 1988, 1993 and 1996, four firm years chosen to lay the difference bare.
