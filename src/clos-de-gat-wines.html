Judean Hills  ·  Israel

The Wines notes for the table

Why these nine

One estate, read across everything it makes. Clos de Gat farm the Ayalon Valley in the Judean foothills west of Jerusalem, dry-farmed on terra rossa over limestone. Two barrel-fermented Chardonnays open; then the Har’el single-varietal range, Syrah in two vintages and a Cabernet; the estate’s Ayalon Valley Bordeaux blend in a three-vintage vertical; and the rare Sycra Merlot 2006 to close.

What to look for

A single house style, followed from white to red and from young to old. Watch the Chardonnays for a Burgundian hand under a warm sun, the Syrah for pepper and dark fruit, and the Cabernet for savoury structure.

Then the Ayalon Valley vertical: the same blend across 2015, 2017 and 2018, with only the vintage changing, before the mature Sycra Merlot shows how a great Clos de Gat red ages.

Each note is drawn from the producer’s own description and critics’ published tasting notes. The community scores are the crowd’s verdict, not ours: Vivino on a five-point scale, CellarTracker out of a hundred. Signposts, not a script; trust your own glass first.

1

Clos de Gat, Chardonnay 2019

Judean Hills · Chardonnay

Judean Hills Chardonnay Barrel-fermented

Clos de Gat farm the Judean foothills by the Ayalon Valley, and their Chardonnay is barrel-fermented in a Burgundian mould. From 2019: pale gold, with orange blossom, citrus, white peach and a subtle spice, fresh and harmonious with balanced oak and a long finish. A serious, food-friendly white.

Community Vivino 4.2★
Look for

Burgundian Chardonnay under a Mediterranean sun: citrus and blossom, balanced oak, fresh.

2

Clos de Gat, Chardonnay 2020

Judean Hills · Chardonnay

Judean Hills Chardonnay Crisp

The same wine one year on, the crisper, more recent release. Bright and elegant: citrus, orchard fruit and a fine mineral thread, with gentle oak and good acidity. A close, community-equal companion to the 2019.

Community Vivino 4.2★
Look for

The fresher, younger Chardonnay, near-identical in style to the 2019.

3

Clos de Gat, ‘Har’el’ Syrah 2018

Judean Hills · Har’el Syrah

Judean Hills Har’el Syrah Peppery

Har’el is Clos de Gat’s single-varietal range; this is their Syrah from the strong 2018. A Mediterranean read of the northern Rhone grape: dark plum and blackberry, black pepper, olive and dried herbs, full and warming with firm tannin and a savoury edge. The riper, more resolved of the two Syrah vintages.

Look for

Warm-climate Syrah: dark fruit and pepper with a savoury, herbal lift.

4

Clos de Gat, ‘Har’el’ Syrah 2022

Judean Hills · Har’el Syrah

Judean Hills Har’el Syrah Youngest

The same Har’el Syrah four years younger, and the night’s most recent release. Bright and primary: fresh blackberry and violet, pepper and a stony core, with firm young tannin still settling. The Syrah caught early, against the more evolved 2018.

Look for

The same wine young: brighter fruit and firmer tannin than the 2018.

5

Clos de Gat, ‘Har’el’ Cabernet Sauvignon 2020

Judean Hills · Har’el Cabernet

Judean Hills Har’el Cabernet Single-varietal

The Har’el Cabernet, the single-varietal bottling, from 2020. Firmer and more structured than the Syrah: blackcurrant, dark cherry, cedar and a herbal, sun-baked depth, with grippy tannin and length. Clos de Gat’s Cabernet on its own, before the Ayalon Valley shows it in a blend.

Look for

Straight Judean Hills Cabernet: cassis and cedar over firm, savoury tannin.

6

Clos de Gat, ‘Ayalon Valley’ 2015

Judean Hills · Cabernet, Merlot, Petit Verdot

Judean Hills Bordeaux blend Vertical

The estate red, a Bordeaux blend of Cabernet, Merlot and Petit Verdot, and the oldest of the three-vintage vertical. From 2015, now with some age: ripe dark berry softening toward plum and cedar, herbs and a warm, savoury depth, tannin resolving. The vertical’s starting point, and a bridge toward the mature Sycra.

Look for

The estate blend with a few years on it: fruit easing into savoury, tannin resolving.

7

Clos de Gat, ‘Ayalon Valley’ 2017

Judean Hills · Cabernet, Merlot, Petit Verdot

Judean Hills Bordeaux blend Warm

The same blend from the warm, excellent 2017: ripe blackberry, plum, herbs and cedar, full and warming with firm tannin and a savoury, sun-baked depth. The middle vintage of the vertical, and the warmest in feel.

Community CellarTracker 92.5
Look for

Judean warmth: a ripe, herbal Bordeaux blend, full and sun-baked.

8

Clos de Gat, ‘Ayalon Valley’ 2018

Judean Hills · Cabernet, Merlot, Petit Verdot

Judean Hills Bordeaux blend Outstanding

The same blend, from the outstanding, top-rated 2018, the estate’s best recent vintage. More complete than the 2017: dense dark fruit, cassis, herbs and spice, with polished tannin and superb balance. The finest of the three Ayalons, and the vertical’s peak.

Community CellarTracker 94
Look for

2018’s balance and depth, the top vintage of the Ayalon vertical.

9

Clos de Gat, ‘Sycra’ Merlot 2006

Judean Hills · single-varietal Merlot

Judean Hills Sycra Merlot Mature bonus

Sycra is Clos de Gat’s rare flagship, made only in the finest vintages; this mature 2006 Merlot is the night’s special bonus. Nearly twenty years on: dried plum, fig, leather, tobacco and cedar, soft and resolved, with a savoury, tertiary finish. A glimpse of how age treats a great Israeli red.

Community CellarTracker 87.8
Look for

Mature Israeli Merlot: dried fruit, leather and cedar over resolved tannin.
