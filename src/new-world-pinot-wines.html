Central Otago  ·  Carneros

The Wines notes for the table

Why these seven

Pinot Noir from the New World, built around three Felton Road single vineyards from Bannockburn, Central Otago, with a natural-wine pair from Sato in Pisa, a Gibbston neighbour and a Carneros foil for contrast. Block 3, Block 5 and Calvert show how one producer reads three sites; Sato’s Pisa Terrace and the estate vineyard above it span 2015 to 2023; Two Paddocks and HDV set place against place.

What to look for

New World Pinot trades Burgundy’s earth for riper fruit and sunlight, but the best sites keep freshness and detail. Central Otago, the world’s southernmost wine region, gives vivid, spicy, structured Pinot; Carneros, cooled by San Francisco fog, a silkier Californian style.

Watch the three Felton Road wines (limestone Block 3, clay Block 5, perfumed Calvert) for how vineyard speaks under one hand, then Bannockburn, Gibbston, Pisa and Carneros for how a change of place changes the tune. The two 2015s, Felton Road and Sato, set conventional against low-intervention in the same year.

Each note is drawn from the producer’s own description and critics’ published tasting notes. The community scores are the crowd’s verdict, not ours: Vivino on a five-point scale, CellarTracker out of a hundred. Signposts, not a script; trust your own glass first.

1

Felton Road, ‘Block 3’ 2015

Bannockburn, Central Otago, New Zealand · Pinot Noir

Central Otago Single-vineyard Mature

Felton Road are Central Otago’s benchmark, farming Bannockburn biodynamically; Block 3 comes from limestone-rich soils in the Elms Vineyard. From the warm 2015, now eleven years on: ripe dark cherry, wild thyme, spice and a savoury depth emerging with age, silky and long. The mature anchor of the Felton Road trio.

Community Vivino 4.3★
Look for

Bottle age on a warm Otago year: dark fruit turning savoury, still fresh.

2

Sato, ‘Pisa Terrace’ Pinot Noir 2015

Pisa, Central Otago, New Zealand · Pinot Noir

Pisa Natural Mature

Yoshiaki and Kyoko Sato left banking for wine, training at Bizot, Pacalet and Felton Road itself before founding Sato in 2009 on bought-in organic fruit. Pisa Terrace, gravelly loam over schist and quartz on the western side of the Cromwell Basin, was their founding site. Wild-fermented with a touch of whole bunch, 19 months in older French oak, minimal sulphur. From the same warm 2015 as the Block 3: red cherry and red fruit, now softening and savoury. The natural-wine counterpoint to Felton Road.

Community Vivino 3.7★
Look for

Same vintage, other hands: a low-intervention 2015 beside Felton Road’s, and what age does to each.

3

HDV, ‘Ygnacia’ Pinot Noir 2018

Los Carneros, California, USA · Pinot Noir

Los Carneros California Silky

HDV is the Napa venture of the Hyde family and the de Villaines of Burgundy’s Domaine de la Romanée-Conti; Ygnacia is their Carneros Pinot. From the phenomenal, generous 2018: ripe red cherry, pomegranate, baking spice and a silky, Burgundian restraint. The Carneros foil, sunlit but poised.

Look for

A Burgundian hand in California: ripe fruit with restraint and silk.

4

Felton Road, ‘Block 5’ 2020

Bannockburn, Central Otago, New Zealand · Pinot Noir

Central Otago Single-vineyard Powerful

Block 5, from deeper clay in the same Elms Vineyard, is Felton Road’s most powerful single-vineyard Pinot. From the concentrated, small-crop 2020: dense black cherry, plum, dark spice and firm, ripe tannin, powerful yet fresh. The muscular heart of the Felton Road trio.

Community Vivino 4.4★
Look for

Block 5’s clay-driven power and 2020’s concentration, the density benchmark of the flight.

5

Felton Road, ‘Calvert’ 2021

Bannockburn, Central Otago, New Zealand · Pinot Noir

Central Otago Calvert Fragrant

A third Felton Road vineyard, Calvert, shared with a handful of top growers, from the outstanding 2021. Fragrant and detailed: red cherry, rose, exotic spice and cracked pepper, medium-bodied with fine tannin and vibrant length. The perfumed counterpoint to the Block wines.

Community Vivino 4.2★
Look for

A different Bannockburn vineyard: perfume and spice over the Blocks’ power.

6

Two Paddocks, ‘First Paddock’ 2021

Gibbston, Central Otago, New Zealand · Pinot Noir

Gibbston Cool-climate Taut

Two Paddocks is the actor Sam Neill’s estate, and First Paddock its original Gibbston vineyard, the coolest, highest corner of Central Otago. From the same 2021 as the Calvert: leaner and more herbal, with red cherry, cranberry, thyme and a whip of acidity, taut and savoury. The Gibbston foil, cool-climate and nervy.

Look for

Gibbston’s cool edge: leaner, herbal, high-acid Otago beside the riper Bannockburn wines.

7

La Ferme de Sato, ‘Sur Les Nuages’ 2023

Mount Pisa, Central Otago, New Zealand · Pinot Noir

Pisa Estate vineyard Natural

La Ferme de Sato is the Satos’ own farm, planted from 2016 directly above the Pisa Terrace vineyard, organic and farmed without chemicals. Wild-fermented without whole bunch, a long maceration, 20 months in French oak (22% new), unfined, unfiltered and bottled with only 15 mg/l of sulphur. James Suckling rates the 2023 96 points: fresh and dried strawberry, cherry and cranberry, orange peel, a hint of smoke and flint, medium-bodied with fine tannin and a juicy line. The other half of the Sato pair.

Look for

One hillside, eight years apart: the young estate wine beside the mature Pisa Terrace below it.
