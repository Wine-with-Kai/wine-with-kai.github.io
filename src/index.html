Wine with Kai

Kai curates and organises wine dinners for people who love wine. You do not need to be an expert to enjoy it; the only thing asked of you is a little curiosity.

Each dinner sets out to explore a different corner of the wine world and to widen the palate, one glass at a time. Every dinner is a small group affair, kept to no more than twelve seats, and all are welcome.
