Golan Heights  ·  Israel

The Wines notes for the table

Why these eight

One house, and its central question: what does a single vineyard add? Yarden is the flagship of the Golan Heights Winery, farming high on the cool, volcanic Golan in Israel’s north. An Allone Habashan Merlot pair opens; then two single-vineyard Syrahs, from Bar’On and Allone Habashan; and a Cabernet flight that sets the estate wine against both single vineyards across two vintages.

What to look for

Altitude and volcanic soil run through everything: ripe fruit held in check by cool nights, with a stony, mineral core. Watch the Merlot pair for the vineyard’s stamp across two years, and the two Syrahs for how Bar’On and Allone Habashan differ.

The Cabernet flight is the heart of the night: estate against single vineyard, and 2017 against 2018, a direct look at how one Golan site concentrates and refines the estate blend.

Each note is drawn from the producer’s own description and critics’ published tasting notes. The community scores are the crowd’s verdict, not ours: Vivino on a five-point scale, CellarTracker out of a hundred. Signposts, not a script; trust your own glass first.

1

Yarden, ‘Allone Habashan’ Merlot 2016

Golan Heights · single-vineyard Merlot

Golan Heights Single-vineyard Merlot

A single-vineyard Merlot from Allone Habashan on the Golan, the older of the pair. Cool, high-altitude Merlot: plum, black cherry and cassis, with herbs, cocoa and a stony core, rounded but firmly structured. The vineyard’s starting point, to read against the 2017 beside it.

Look for

Golan Merlot with grip: dark plum and herbs over a cool, stony frame.

2

Yarden, ‘Allone Habashan’ Merlot 2017

Golan Heights · single-vineyard Merlot

Golan Heights Single-vineyard Merlot

The same vineyard a year on, from the excellent, clean 2017. A shade riper and more polished: sweet plum and dark berry, mocha and a fine herbal lift, with supple tannin. The single-vineyard Merlot across two vintages, one site and grape.

Look for

2017’s riper polish against the 2016, the same vineyard’s vintage shift.

3

Yarden, ‘Bar’On’ Syrah 2017

Golan Heights · single-vineyard Syrah

Golan Heights Single-vineyard Syrah

Single-vineyard Syrah from Bar’On, in the excellent 2017. Cool-climate Syrah with real lift: blackberry and dark plum, cracked pepper, violet and smoked meat, with a stony, mineral length. The first of two Golan sites for the grape.

Look for

High-altitude Syrah: dark fruit and pepper with a floral, mineral edge.

4

Yarden, ‘Allone Habashan’ Syrah 2018

Golan Heights · single-vineyard Syrah

Golan Heights Single-vineyard Syrah

A second single-vineyard Syrah, from Allone Habashan in the strong 2018, the same site as the Merlot pair. Firmer and more savoury than the Bar’On: dark berry, black olive, pepper and dried herbs, with grippy tannin and a stony core. Two Golan vineyards, one grape.

Look for

A different vineyard’s Syrah: savoury and firm against Bar’On’s lift.

5

Yarden, Cabernet Sauvignon 2017

Golan Heights · Cabernet Sauvignon

Golan Heights Cabernet Estate

The estate Cabernet from the excellent, warm-but-clean 2017: cassis, black cherry, cedar, mint and a touch of graphite, ripe and structured with firm tannin. A polished style where the New World meets Bordeaux, and the reference point for the two single vineyards.

Community Vivino 4.3★
Look for

Cool, high-altitude Cabernet: ripe cassis and mint with real structure.

6

Yarden, ‘Bar’On’ Cabernet Sauvignon 2017

Golan Heights · single-vineyard Cabernet

Golan Heights Single-vineyard Concentrated

The same 2017, from the single Bar’On Vineyard, a step up in concentration and detail. Denser and more mineral: blackcurrant, dark cherry, tobacco and volcanic stone, with firm, fine tannin and length. Estate against single vineyard, one grape and year.

Look for

How a single Golan vineyard concentrates and refines the estate Cabernet.

7

Yarden, Cabernet Sauvignon 2018

Golan Heights · Cabernet Sauvignon

Golan Heights Cabernet Estate

The estate Cabernet again, from the strong, highly rated 2018. A shade riper and rounder than the 2017: ripe cassis, plum, cedar and sweet spice, with supple tannin. The estate wine across two vintages.

Community Vivino 4.2★
Look for

2018’s rounder ripeness against 2017, the estate Cabernet’s vintage shift.

8

Yarden, ‘Allone Habashan’ Cabernet Sauvignon 2018

Golan Heights · single-vineyard Cabernet

Golan Heights Single-vineyard Savoury

The 2018 single-vineyard, from Allone Habashan on the Golan. Firm and savoury: blackcurrant, herbs, graphite and a stony core, structured and age-worthy. A second single vineyard to set against Bar’On and the estate wine, and the close of the flight.

Look for

A different Golan vineyard’s stamp: firm, stony, savoury Cabernet.
