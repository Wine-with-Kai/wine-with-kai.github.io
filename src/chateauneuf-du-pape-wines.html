Southern Rhône  ·  Grenache

The Wines notes for the table

Why these eight

The southern Rhône’s grandest name, across five houses and a three-vintage Clos des Papes vertical. Grenache-led blends from the galets of Châteauneuf, from an entry cuvée to the appellation’s benchmark estate.

What to look for

Châteauneuf-du-Pape is warm, Grenache-driven and generous: kirsch, garrigue (wild herbs), pepper and sweet spice, high in alcohol but savoury with it.

Watch the house styles (oak-framed Laurus, traditional Maucoil, the Beaucastel-family Sinards, benchmark Clos des Papes), and follow the Clos des Papes vertical (2014, 2018, 2019) for the vintages.

Each note is drawn from the producer’s own description and critics’ published tasting notes. The community scores are the crowd’s verdict, not ours: Vivino on a five-point scale, CellarTracker out of a hundred. Signposts, not a script; trust your own glass first.

1

Clos des Papes, Paul Avril 2014

Châteauneuf-du-Pape · Grenache-led blend

Châteauneuf-du-Pape Clos des Papes Fragrant

Clos des Papes, run by the Avril family, is Châteauneuf’s benchmark estate, a Grenache-based blend of many varieties. From the fresher, earlier 2014: red cherry, raspberry, garrigue and white pepper, medium-full and fragrant with supple tannin. The vertical’s most approachable vintage.

Community CellarTracker 92.4
Look for

Benchmark Châteauneuf in a fresher year: kirsch, garrigue and pepper, supple.

2

Laurus, Châteauneuf-du-Pape 2015

Gabriel Meffre · Grenache-led blend

Châteauneuf-du-Pape Oak-framed Plush

Laurus is Gabriel Meffre’s premium range, richer and more oak-framed than the traditional style. From the ripe, structured 2015: black cherry, plum, cocoa, sweet spice and vanilla, full-bodied and plush with firm tannin. A modern, polished Châteauneuf.

Look for

The modern, oaked style: dark fruit, cocoa and spice, full and plush.

3

Laurus, Châteauneuf-du-Pape 2016

Gabriel Meffre · Grenache-led blend

Châteauneuf-du-Pape Oak-framed Outstanding

The same cuvée from the outstanding, near-perfect 2016, the finest recent southern Rhône vintage. Deeper and more complete: ripe black cherry, kirsch, garrigue, chocolate and spice, concentrated with polished tannin and freshness. The peak of the two Laurus.

Look for

2016’s concentration and balance, the better of the two Laurus.

4

Château Maucoil, ‘L’Esprit de Maucoil’ 2017

Châteauneuf-du-Pape · Grenache-led blend

Châteauneuf-du-Pape Traditional Savoury

Château Maucoil is a long-established, largely organic estate in a more traditional mould; L’Esprit is its classic cuvée. From the warm, concentrated 2017: kirsch, red plum, garrigue, liquorice and pepper, warming and savoury with soft tannin. Traditional Châteauneuf warmth.

Look for

The traditional style: warm kirsch and garrigue, savoury and soft.

5

Château Maucoil, ‘Privilège’ 2017

Châteauneuf-du-Pape · old-vine Grenache

Châteauneuf-du-Pape Prestige cuvée Concentrated

Maucoil’s prestige cuvée, Privilège, from the same 2017, a step up in old-vine concentration. Deeper and more structured: dark cherry, fig, garrigue, spice and a mineral core, fuller with firmer tannin. Estate cuvée against prestige, one vintage.

Look for

Prestige against classic: Privilège’s old-vine depth over L’Esprit.

6

Famille Perrin, ‘Les Sinards’ 2018

Châteauneuf-du-Pape · Grenache-led blend

Châteauneuf-du-Pape Beaucastel family Supple

Les Sinards is the Perrin family’s Châteauneuf, made with fruit from around their famed Château de Beaucastel. From the ripe, generous 2018: red and black cherry, garrigue, pepper and sweet spice, supple and fragrant with gentle tannin. An accessible, family-signature Châteauneuf.

Look for

The Beaucastel touch at an accessible level: fragrant, peppery, supple.

7

Clos des Papes, Paul Avril 2018

Châteauneuf-du-Pape · Grenache-led blend

Châteauneuf-du-Pape Clos des Papes Powerful

Clos des Papes returns in the ripe, powerful 2018. Denser and more structured than the 2014: black cherry, kirsch, garrigue, liquorice and spice, concentrated with firm tannin and length. The vertical’s muscular middle.

Look for

2018’s density and structure, the benchmark estate at full weight.

8

Clos des Papes, Paul Avril 2019

Châteauneuf-du-Pape · Grenache-led blend

Châteauneuf-du-Pape Clos des Papes Acclaimed

The finale, Clos des Papes in the superb, acclaimed 2019, one of its greatest recent years. Ripe yet fresh: kirsch, blackberry, garrigue, pepper and floral spice, concentrated and vibrant with fine, ripe tannin and a very long finish. The peak of the vertical and the night.

Community CellarTracker 95.3
Look for

The vertical’s summit: 2019’s ripe, fresh concentration, the finest of the three.
