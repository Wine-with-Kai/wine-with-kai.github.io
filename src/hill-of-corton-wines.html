Aloxe-Corton  ·  Grand Cru  ·  Burgundy

The Wines notes for the table

Why these seven

One Grand Cru hillside, read across climats, producers and a decade. A premier-cru warm-up from Aloxe-Corton opens; then six Corton Grand Crus, with a De Montille vertical (2010 and 2012), a Rapet vertical (2012 and 2020), a 2012 face-off between the two houses, and the hand of five different growers on one famous hill.

What to look for

Corton is the Côte de Beaune’s largest Grand Cru, and its Pinot Noir is the most structured and savoury of the region: red and dark cherry, iron, forest floor and firm, fine tannin, built to age.

Watch three things: the climat (aristocratic Clos du Roi against eastern Le Rognet and the broader hill), the producer’s hand (De Montille’s ethereal, whole-cluster style; Latour’s classicism; Rapet’s earthier depth), and the vintage running 2010 through 2022.

Each note is drawn from the producer’s own description and critics’ published tasting notes. The community scores are the crowd’s verdict, not ours: Vivino on a five-point scale, CellarTracker out of a hundred. Signposts, not a script; trust your own glass first.

1

Albert Bichot, Aloxe-Corton 1er ‘Clos des Maréchaudes’ 2022

Aloxe-Corton, Côte de Beaune · Pinot Noir

Aloxe-Corton 1er Pinot Noir Warm-up

The warm-up, from the premier-cru slope just below the Grand Cru. A generous, outstanding 2022: bright red cherry and raspberry, violets and sweet spice, supple and juicy with fine tannin and real freshness. Approachable now, it sets the hill’s baseline before the Grand Crus climb above it.

Look for

The premier-cru voice of the hill, fruit-forward and open, a reference point for the Grand Crus to follow.

2

De Montille, Corton ‘Clos du Roi’ Grand Cru 2010

Aloxe-Corton, Côte de Beaune · Pinot Noir

Corton Grand Cru Clos du Roi Structured

Domaine de Montille farm biodynamically and favour ethereal, whole-cluster reds; Clos du Roi is Corton’s most aristocratic climat. From the structured, high-acid 2010, it is tightly wound and savoury: red and black cherry, blood orange, iron and forest floor over firm, fine tannin. Built for the long haul, and only now beginning to unfurl.

Look for

Aristocratic Corton in a classic, age-worthy year: savoury depth and fine, firm tannin.

3

De Montille, Corton ‘Clos du Roi’ Grand Cru 2012

Aloxe-Corton, Côte de Beaune · Pinot Noir

Corton Grand Cru Clos du Roi Vertical

The same climat and cellar, from the small, concentrated 2012. Rounder and more immediately giving than the 2010: darker cherry and cassis, spice and a floral lift, with soft, ripe tannin and real depth for the year. Poured beside the 2010, the estate’s style held constant while the season speaks.

Look for

The vertical’s second half: 2012’s soft-tannin concentration against the firmer, tauter 2010.

4

Rapet Père et Fils, Corton Grand Cru 2012

Aloxe-Corton, Côte de Beaune · Pinot Noir

Corton Grand Cru Rapet 2012 face-off

Domaine Rapet, a Pernand-Vergelesses family estate with old holdings on the hill, from the same 2012 as the De Montille beside it. A broader, more open corner of Corton: ripe red fruit, undergrowth, sweet spice and a gentle earthiness, with supple tannin and a savoury finish. The night’s face-off, one vintage across two houses and two parts of the hill.

Look for

Same year, different grower and climat: how much of Corton is site, and how much is cellar.

5

Louis Latour, Corton Grand Cru 2014

Aloxe-Corton, Côte de Beaune · Pinot Noir

Corton Grand Cru Louis Latour Classical

Louis Latour is Corton’s largest landholder, and their red is a classical benchmark. From the fresh, small-crop 2014: elegant and mid-weight, with red cherry, rose, tea leaf and a fine mineral thread, the tannins polished and bright. Less brooding than the 2010s, a poised and drinkable expression of the hill.

Look for

The classical, polished style of Corton’s biggest name, in a fresh and elegant year.

6

Michel Mallard, Corton ‘Le Rognet’ Grand Cru 2019

Ladoix, Côte de Beaune · Pinot Noir

Corton Grand Cru Le Rognet Ripe

Domaine Michel Mallard farm the Le Rognet climat on the Ladoix side of the hill, from the ripe, acclaimed 2019. Fuller and more sumptuous: black cherry, cassis, violet and warm spice, generous fruit over firm but ripe tannin. Modern, concentrated Corton from a great recent vintage, with the structure to age.

Look for

A warmer climat and vintage: sumptuous black fruit over firm, ripe tannin.

7

Rapet Père et Fils, Corton Grand Cru 2020

Aloxe-Corton, Côte de Beaune · Pinot Noir

Corton Grand Cru Rapet Vertical

Rapet again, closing the flight with the superb, concentrated 2020. Deep and vibrant: ripe dark cherry, blood orange, spice and a stony, savoury core, fresh acidity keeping the ripeness taut. The estate’s second vertical vintage, and one of the finest recent years on the hill.

Look for

Rapet’s 2012 and 2020 vertical completed: the concentration and freshness of a great year, as the finale.
