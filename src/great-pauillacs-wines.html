Pauillac  ·  Haut-Médoc

The Wines notes for the table

Why these eight

One commune, eight classified growths. Two mature super-seconds open, the Pichon pair; a three-château 2014 horizontal runs through the middle; and three younger fifth growths close, across the heart of Cabernet Sauvignon.

What to look for

Pauillac is the Médoc’s Cabernet stronghold: firm and structured, with cassis and cedar, graphite and pencil-lead, built to age for decades.

Watch the hierarchy (the super-seconds’ extra depth and polish over the fifth growths), the 2014 horizontal (three houses, one year, side by side), and how the mature 1998 and 2008 drink beside the youngsters.

Each note is drawn from the producer’s own description and critics’ published tasting notes. The community scores are the crowd’s verdict, not ours: Vivino on a five-point scale, CellarTracker out of a hundred. Signposts, not a script; trust your own glass first.

1

Château Pichon-Longueville Baron 1998

Pauillac · Second Growth

Second Growth Pichon Baron Mature

Pichon-Longueville Baron makes Pauillac’s most muscular super-second, and this 1998 is now mature. Powerful yet resolving: cassis, cedar, tobacco, leather and graphite, with softening tannin and a long, savoury finish. The elder statesman of the flight.

Look for

A mature super-second: power softening into cedar, leather and graphite.

2

Château Pichon-Longueville Comtesse de Lalande 2008

Pauillac · Second Growth

Second Growth Comtesse Graceful

Pichon Comtesse, across the road from the Baron, is the more perfumed and graceful of the two, with a dash of Merlot. From the fresh, underrated 2008, drinking beautifully: cassis, violet, cedar and a silky, aromatic depth, with fine tannin. The elegant counterpoint to the Baron.

Community Vivino 4.4★
Look for

The Comtesse’s perfume and silk against the Baron’s power, both super-seconds now mature.

3

Château Grand-Puy-Ducasse 2014

Pauillac · Fifth Growth

Fifth Growth 2014 horizontal Approachable

Grand-Puy-Ducasse, a fifth growth in a gentler, more forward style, opens the 2014 horizontal. Classic and fresh: cassis, red cherry, cedar and pencil shavings, medium-bodied with fine, firm tannin. The most approachable corner of the trio.

Look for

The gentler Grand-Puy: classic Pauillac fruit at an approachable weight.

4

Château Grand-Puy-Lacoste 2014

Pauillac · Fifth Growth

Fifth Growth 2014 horizontal Classic

Grand-Puy-Lacoste is the quintessential fifth growth, pure Pauillac Cabernet with a devoted following. From 2014: cassis, blackcurrant, cedar and graphite, firm and classically structured, with excellent length. The benchmark of the horizontal.

Community Vivino 4.3★
Look for

Textbook Pauillac: cassis, cedar and graphite over firm, fine tannin.

5

Château Lynch-Bages 2014

Pauillac · Fifth Growth

Fifth Growth Super-fifth Rich

Lynch-Bages, the ’super-fifth’, is the richest of the trio, always denser than its rank. From 2014: ripe cassis, black cherry, cedar, tobacco and spice, full and structured with velvety tannin. The most powerful 2014 of the three.

Community Vivino 4.4★
Look for

The super-fifth’s extra flesh and depth, the richest of the 2014 horizontal.

6

Château Batailley 2016

Pauillac · Fifth Growth

Fifth Growth Traditional Outstanding

Château Batailley is a sturdy, traditional fifth growth and fine value, from the outstanding 2016. Firm and classical: cassis, dark cherry, cedar and earth, with grippy tannin and a fresh, structured finish. Old-school Pauillac in a great year.

Community Vivino 4.3★
Look for

Sturdy, classical Pauillac from a top vintage, structure and value.

7

Château Pontet-Canet 2018

Pauillac · Fifth Growth

Fifth Growth Biodynamic Powerful

Pontet-Canet, biodynamic and cult-followed, drinks at classed-growth-plus level. From the ripe, powerful 2018: dense cassis, blackberry, violet, graphite and spice, concentrated with fine, ripe tannin and a long finish. One of the flight’s stars.

Community Vivino 4.4★
Look for

Cult Pauillac: concentration and polish, fifth growth in name only.

8

Château Clerc Milon 2019

Pauillac · Fifth Growth

Fifth Growth Rothschild Modern

Clerc Milon, a Rothschild property beside Mouton, closes the flight from the superb 2019. Aromatic and modern: cassis, dark cherry, violet, cocoa and spice, ripe and balanced with fine tannin and freshness. The youngest and most flamboyant wine of the night.

Community Vivino 4.2★
Look for

The Mouton neighbour in a great modern year: aromatic, ripe, balanced.
