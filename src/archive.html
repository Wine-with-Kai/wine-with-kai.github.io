Past Dinners the themes so far

Every dinner takes a single idea and follows it across the table. A region set against its neighbour, one grape read across the decades, a great house shown beside a newcomer. The evenings are gathered here as they pass.

Napa Valley California

Eight Cabernets of Napa Valley

Cabernet Sauvignon · Blind · The current dinner

Eight bottles, six vintages, one grape. The valley’s icons from the Atlas Peak heights to the Rutherford benches, poured blind and set out by vintage, from a mature 2012 to the ripe 2019s. A single grape read across a decade of Napa.

2012 to 2019 Eight bottles Poured blind

Thursday 1 October, So Good Seafood, Takashimaya · invitation and notes

Northern Italy Piedmont & the Veneto

Kings of the North

Nebbiolo and Corvina · Barolo against Amarone

Four bottles, two houses: two Barolos from Boroli set against two Amarones from Cesari. Piedmont’s Nebbiolo, all perfume and grip, against the Veneto’s dark, dried-grape richness, Italy’s noble north poured side by side.

Barolo Amarone Four bottles Two houses

Thursday 10 September, So Good Seafood, Takashimaya · invitation and notes

Bordeaux Right Bank

Pomerol versus Saint-Émilion

Merlot and Cabernet Franc · Blind

Eight bottles, four vintages, the two great Right Bank neighbours set directly against each other in matched pairs and poured blind. Pomerol’s iron-rich clay against Saint-Émilion’s limestone, judged glass by glass across 1986, 1988, 1993 and 1996, four firm years chosen to lay the difference bare.

1986 1988 1993 1996 Matched pairs
