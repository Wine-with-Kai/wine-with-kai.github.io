Italy Piedmont · Tuscany · Veneto

Simply Italian

Barolo · Brunello · Amarone

Three of Italy’s greats side by side: Barolo, built on Piedmont’s Nebbiolo; Brunello, the pride of Sangiovese in Tuscany; and Amarone, the Veneto’s rich dried-grape red. A fine way to taste the differences and subtleties between these well-known names.
