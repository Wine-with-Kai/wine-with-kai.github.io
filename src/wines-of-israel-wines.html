Golan Heights  ·  Judean Hills

The Wines notes for the table

Why these nine

An introduction to Israeli wine from two regions and two estates. Two Clos de Gat Chardonnays open; then Yarden’s Golan Heights Cabernets, estate and single-vineyard, in two vintages; two vintages of Clos de Gat’s Ayalon Valley red; and a mature Sycra Merlot to close.

What to look for

Two very different terroirs sit side by side. Yarden farms high on the cool, volcanic Golan Heights in the north; Clos de Gat the warmer Judean Hills near Jerusalem.

Watch the whites for freshness, the Cabernets for how single vineyards concentrate the estate blend, and the reds for Mediterranean warmth held in check by altitude, before a mature Merlot shows how it all ages.

Each note is drawn from the producer’s own description and critics’ published tasting notes. The community scores are the crowd’s verdict, not ours: Vivino on a five-point scale, CellarTracker out of a hundred. Signposts, not a script; trust your own glass first.

1

Clos de Gat, Chardonnay 2019

Judean Hills · Chardonnay

Judean Hills Chardonnay Barrel-fermented

Clos de Gat farm the Judean foothills by the Ayalon Valley, and their Chardonnay is barrel-fermented in a Burgundian mould. From 2019: pale gold, with orange blossom, citrus, white peach and a subtle spice, fresh and harmonious with balanced oak and a long finish. A serious, food-friendly white.

Community Vivino 4.2★
Look for

Burgundian Chardonnay under a Mediterranean sun: citrus and blossom, balanced oak, fresh.

2

Clos de Gat, Chardonnay 2020

Judean Hills · Chardonnay

Judean Hills Chardonnay Crisp

The same wine one year on, the crisper, more recent release. Bright and elegant: citrus, orchard fruit and a fine mineral thread, with gentle oak and good acidity. A close, community-equal companion to the 2019.

Community Vivino 4.2★
Look for

The fresher, younger Chardonnay, near-identical in style to the 2019.

3

Yarden, Cabernet Sauvignon 2017

Golan Heights · Cabernet Sauvignon

Golan Heights Cabernet Estate

Yarden is the flagship of the Golan Heights Winery, high on cool volcanic soils. The estate Cabernet from the excellent, warm-but-clean 2017: cassis, black cherry, cedar, mint and a touch of graphite, ripe and structured with firm tannin. A polished style where the New World meets Bordeaux.

Community Vivino 4.3★
Look for

Cool, high-altitude Cabernet: ripe cassis and mint with real structure.

4

Yarden, ‘Bar’On’ Cabernet Sauvignon 2017

Golan Heights · single-vineyard Cabernet

Golan Heights Single-vineyard Concentrated

The same 2017, from the single Bar’On Vineyard, a step up in concentration and detail. Denser and more mineral: blackcurrant, dark cherry, tobacco and volcanic stone, with firm, fine tannin and length. Estate against single vineyard, one grape and year.

Look for

How a single Golan vineyard concentrates and refines the estate Cabernet.

5

Yarden, Cabernet Sauvignon 2018

Golan Heights · Cabernet Sauvignon

Golan Heights Cabernet Estate

The estate Cabernet again, from the strong, highly rated 2018. A shade riper and rounder than the 2017: ripe cassis, plum, cedar and sweet spice, with supple tannin. The estate wine across two vintages.

Community Vivino 4.2★
Look for

2018’s rounder ripeness against 2017, the estate Cabernet’s vintage shift.

6

Yarden, ‘Allone Habashan’ Cabernet Sauvignon 2018

Golan Heights · single-vineyard Cabernet

Golan Heights Single-vineyard Savoury

The 2018 single-vineyard, from Allone Habashan on the Golan. Firm and savoury: blackcurrant, herbs, graphite and a stony core, structured and age-worthy. A second single vineyard to set against Bar’On and the estate wine.

Look for

A different Golan vineyard’s stamp: firm, stony, savoury Cabernet.

7

Clos de Gat, ‘Ayalon Valley’ 2017

Judean Hills · Cabernet, Merlot, Petit Verdot

Judean Hills Bordeaux blend Warm

Clos de Gat’s estate red, a Bordeaux blend of Cabernet, Merlot and Petit Verdot from the warmer Judean Hills. From the warm, excellent 2017: ripe blackberry, plum, herbs and cedar, full and warming with firm tannin and a savoury, sun-baked depth. The southern counterpoint to the Golan Cabernets.

Community CellarTracker 92.5
Look for

Judean warmth: a ripe, herbal Bordeaux blend against the cool Golan wines.

8

Clos de Gat, ‘Ayalon Valley’ 2018

Judean Hills · Cabernet, Merlot, Petit Verdot

Judean Hills Bordeaux blend Outstanding

The same blend, from the outstanding, top-rated 2018, the estate’s best recent vintage. More complete than the 2017: dense dark fruit, cassis, herbs and spice, with polished tannin and superb balance. The finer of the two Ayalons.

Community CellarTracker 94
Look for

2018’s balance and depth, the top vintage of the Ayalon pair.

9

Clos de Gat, ‘Sycra’ Merlot 2006

Judean Hills · single-varietal Merlot

Judean Hills Sycra Merlot Mature bonus

Sycra is Clos de Gat’s rare flagship, made only in the finest vintages; this mature 2006 Merlot is the night’s special bonus. Nearly twenty years on: dried plum, fig, leather, tobacco and cedar, soft and resolved, with a savoury, tertiary finish. A glimpse of how age treats a great Israeli red.

Community CellarTracker 87.8
Look for

Mature Israeli Merlot: dried fruit, leather and cedar over resolved tannin.
