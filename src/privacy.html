Privacywhat we keep, and why

Wine with Kai is a small circle of private wine dinners in Singapore, run by Kai. When you reserve a seat or add a bottle on this site, we keep only what is needed to run the evening. This page explains what that is. Questions, or a request to see or remove your details: winewithkai@gmail.com.

What we keep

Your name and email address. You give these when you sign in, either by email (we send a one-time code) or with Google. Signing in with Google shares only your name, email address and profile picture with us; we do not receive your password or anything else from your Google account.

Your mobile number, only if you choose to add it.

Your reservations: which evenings you reserved, joined the waitlist for, or cancelled. Where a deposit applies, payment is handled by Stripe; we see that it was paid or refunded, never your card or bank details.

The bottles you add: the wine, the price you share it for, a note if you leave one, and the label photo if you take one, together with the market price and short background that our bottle assistant looks up.

How it is used

Only to run the evenings: to hold your seat, to build the line-up, to split the evening’s costs fairly, and to contact you about a dinner you have reserved. We do not sell your details, share them for marketing, or send newsletters.

Who sees what

The line-up for an evening is public, showing each bottle with the first name of the guest bringing it, and its label photo. The tally and the prices are shown only to the guests seated that evening. Kai, as host, sees the guest list with names, email addresses and mobile numbers.

Where it is kept

Your details are stored with Supabase, in its Singapore region. Sign-in codes are sent by email through Gmail. When you photograph a label, the photo is read by Anthropic’s Claude, which also searches the web for the bottle’s market price and background. Label photos are stored at a web address that anyone with the link can open, so they can appear in the line-up.

Keeping and removing

We keep your details while you are part of the dinners. Email winewithkai@gmail.com at any time and we will show you what we hold, correct it, or delete it.
