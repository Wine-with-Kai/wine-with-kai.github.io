Tuscany  ·  Bolgheri

The Wines notes for the table

Why these seven

The rule-breakers of Tuscany, from three estates. A Tignanello vertical (2007 and 2018) frames the night, three vintages of Sette Ponti’s Oreno run through the middle, and two Sassicaia bookend the Bolgheri coast.

What to look for

Super Tuscans set Bordeaux grapes, Cabernet and Merlot, alongside or in place of Sangiovese, and age them in French barrique. Sassicaia is Cabernet-led and Médoc-like; Tignanello is Sangiovese with Cabernet, keeping a Tuscan savour; Oreno is a modern, Merlot-led blend.

Watch the grape balance and the oak, and follow the two verticals (Tignanello 2007 and 2018, the run of Orenos) for how the estates and vintages differ.

Each note is drawn from the producer’s own description and critics’ published tasting notes. The community scores are the crowd’s verdict, not ours: Vivino on a five-point scale, CellarTracker out of a hundred. Signposts, not a script; trust your own glass first.

1

Antinori, ‘Tignanello’ 2007

Toscana IGT, Tuscany · Sangiovese and Cabernet

Toscana IGT Sangiovese-Cabernet Mature

Antinori’s Tignanello is the wine that launched the Super Tuscan movement, Sangiovese with Cabernet aged in barrique. From the ripe, elegant 2007, now mature: dried cherry, leather, tobacco and sweet spice, savoury and silky with resolved tannin and a long, Tuscan-bittersweet finish. The aged opener.

Look for

Tignanello with age: dried fruit and leather over Sangiovese’s savoury grip.

2

Tenuta San Guido, ‘Sassicaia’ 2013

Bolgheri, Tuscany · Cabernet Sauvignon and Cabernet Franc

Bolgheri Cabernet-led Classic

Sassicaia, the original Super Tuscan, is Cabernet-led from Bolgheri on the Tuscan coast, and drinks like a great Médoc. From the classic, elegant 2013: cassis, blackcurrant leaf, cedar, graphite and Mediterranean herbs, with fine tannin and a saline, savoury length. Bordeaux by the sea.

Community Vivino 4.6★
Look for

Médoc in Tuscany: cassis, cedar and herbs with fine, saline tannin.

3

Tenuta Sette Ponti, ‘Oreno’ 2015

Toscana IGT, Tuscany · Merlot, Cabernet, Petit Verdot

Toscana IGT Merlot-led Plush

Sette Ponti’s Oreno is a modern, Merlot-led Bordeaux blend from inland Tuscany. From the warm, generous 2015: ripe black cherry, plum, cocoa and vanilla, plush and full-bodied with velvety tannin. Polished and crowd-pleasing, the first of a three-vintage run.

Community CellarTracker 92.5
Look for

Modern Tuscan opulence: ripe dark fruit, cocoa and velvet, Merlot-led.

4

Tenuta Sette Ponti, ‘Oreno’ 2016

Toscana IGT, Tuscany · Merlot, Cabernet, Petit Verdot

Toscana IGT Merlot-led Balanced

The same wine in the outstanding 2016, Tuscany’s finest recent vintage. More structured and complete than the 2015: black cherry, cassis, graphite and spice, with fine-grained tannin and superb balance. The peak of the Oreno run.

Community CellarTracker 92.6
Look for

2016’s balance and structure, the best of the three Orenos.

5

Antinori, ‘Tignanello’ 2018

Toscana IGT, Tuscany · Sangiovese and Cabernet

Toscana IGT Sangiovese-Cabernet Youthful

Tignanello again, from the fresh, classical 2018, poured against the mature 2007. Vivid and youthful: red and black cherry, violet, tobacco and sweet spice, with bright acidity and firm, fine tannin, Sangiovese’s savour still front and centre. The vertical’s young half.

Community CellarTracker 92.9
Look for

Young Tignanello: vivid fruit and Sangiovese grip against the aged 2007.

6

Tenuta Sette Ponti, ‘Oreno’ 2019

Toscana IGT, Tuscany · Merlot, Cabernet, Petit Verdot

Toscana IGT Merlot-led Superb

The last of the Orenos, from the superb 2019. Ripe yet fresh: dense black cherry, plum, mocha and spice, concentrated with polished tannin and real lift. A top vintage for the modern Tuscan style.

Community CellarTracker 94
Look for

2019’s ripe concentration with freshness, the richest Oreno.

7

Tenuta San Guido, ‘Sassicaia’ 2019

Bolgheri, Tuscany · Cabernet Sauvignon and Cabernet Franc

Bolgheri Cabernet-led Near-perfect

Sassicaia closes the night, from the near-perfect 2019, one of the estate’s greatest recent years. Cassis, cedar, graphite, dark cherry and herbs, powerful yet refined, with silky tannin and a long, saline finish. The Cabernet-led coast at its finest.

Community Vivino 4.5★
Look for

Sassicaia in a benchmark year: power and refinement, the finale.
